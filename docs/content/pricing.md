## Copy

### 1 — Intro

**Heading:** Straight pricing. No "request a quote" runaround.
**Body:** Most developers make you book a call just to hear a number. I'd rather just tell you.
Below is what a redesign actually costs with me — real ranges, all-in, no surprise line items.
Where you land in a range comes down to how many pages you need and how much of the writing and
setup you want me to handle. After a quick look at your site, I'll give you one fixed number.
**CTA:** Request a Website Review · *Not sure which fits? Send me your site and I'll tell you — free.*

### 2 — Project packages  ·  *Pricing Component*

**Website Refresh — $2,500–$3,500**
*For a site that's basically fine but looks dated — a facelift, not a teardown.*
Best for a small business whose current site mostly needs to look current again.
- 1–3 pages
- Homepage redesign
- Mobile cleanup
- Basic copy cleanup
- Contact form
- CTA improvements
- Basic SEO titles & meta
- Launch support

**Small Business Redesign — $5,000–$7,500**  ·  **Most popular**
*The full rebuild most businesses actually need — new design, new structure, words that work.*
Best for contractors, consultants, wellness practices, nonprofits, and local service businesses.
- 5–7 pages
- Homepage redesign
- Service pages
- About page
- Contact / request-a-quote page
- Copywriting assistance
- Mobile-first redesign
- Local SEO structure
- Speed & performance cleanup
- Basic on-page SEO
- 301 redirects (if I'm replacing an old site)
- Launch checklist

**Premium Redesign + Growth Setup — $9,500–$15,000**
*For when you want the site to do more than look good — you want it to bring in work.*
Best for businesses ready to invest in strategy, content, and conversion.
- 8–15 pages
- Strategy session
- Full content rewrite
- Conversion-focused service pages
- Local SEO landing pages
- Portfolio / case-study setup
- Blog / resource structure
- Analytics setup
- CRM / email integration
- Booking or quote-request system
- Redirect map
- 30-day post-launch support

> Card CTA on each tier: **Request a Website Review** (not "Buy now" — the review comes first).

### 3 — Care Plans  ·  *Pricing Component (ongoing)*

**Heading:** Then I keep it running.
**Body:** A website isn't "done" at launch — it needs hosting, updates, and a human when
something breaks. Pick a Care Plan and that's handled too. Month to month, cancel anytime, and
your files are always yours.

- **Basic Care — $150–$250/mo** — Hosting, backups, software updates, and uptime monitoring. The lights stay on, the site stays safe.
- **Growth Care — $350–$500/mo** — Everything in Basic, plus small monthly edits — new photos, copy tweaks, a page here and there. Just text me.
- **Partner Care — $750–$1,500/mo** — Ongoing design, development, and copy. Basically your part-time web team, on call.

### 4 — Pricing FAQ  ·  *FAQ Component*

**Why ranges instead of one price?** Because no two sites are the same. The range covers page
count and how much writing/setup you hand me. After a quick look at your current site, you get
one fixed number — and I stick to it.

**What if I'm between tiers?** Then we talk. I'll point you to whichever gets you what you
actually need, not the bigger one.

**Do I have to get a Care Plan?** No. It's there because most people would rather never think
about hosting and updates again. Skip it, and your site is still 100% yours.

**Who owns the website?** You do — always. The design, the files, the domain, the hosting login.
Walk away whenever you want and take it all with you.

**Do you write the copy?** Yes. Copywriting is built into the project tiers — send me bullet
points and I'll turn them into real pages. No surprise "content fee."
