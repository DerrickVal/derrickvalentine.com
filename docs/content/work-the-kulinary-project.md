## Story

- **Client:** Chef Khari Bell — a DMV chef of 18 years (celebrities, pro athletes, top restaurants).
- **Brief:** a site that reflected a new brand identity and repositioned him as *more than a
  cooking service* — educating customers on new offerings.
- **Result:** rebranded the entire site; demand rose so sharply he had to **pause applications
  for his summer camp**.
- **Scope:** Brand Strategy, UX Strategy · UI/UX Design, Art Direction.
