## What it is

**Mediation & Conflict Resolution Center of Howard County (MCRC)** — a nonprofit offering
mediation, conflict-resolution services, and community education. The redesign replaced a dated,
sparse WordPress site (light blue/orange, broken footer image) with a modern one: dark hero
("Conflict is a part of life — but it doesn't divide us"), a clear "Our Approach" + services
layout, an events/stats band, and Request-Mediation / Become-a-Volunteer paths.
