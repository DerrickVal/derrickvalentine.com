## Story

- **Client:** Uniting Our Youth, Inc. — a 501(c)(3) nonprofit in DC (Wards 5 & 1), 20 years
  steering kids away from gang involvement toward education and mentoring. ED: **Clarence Miles**.
- **Brief:** a site that works two ways — a conversion tool for donors/volunteers, and an
  information tool for parents and grant funders.
- **Result:** rewrote the site copy and reworked the color palette → **an increase in donations
  and user satisfaction**.
- **Scope:** Copywriting, Ecommerce · UI/UX Design, Art Direction.
