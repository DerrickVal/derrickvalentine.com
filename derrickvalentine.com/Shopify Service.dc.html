Questions, answered

[  FAQ  ]

Will you work with my existing theme?

Usually, yes. If it’s a solid base I’ll build on it; if it’s holding you back, I’ll tell you straight and price the alternative.

Can you migrate my products?

Yes — from WooCommerce, Squarespace, Wix, BigCommerce and more, with redirects so you don’t lose rankings. Add it in the calculator.

Do you handle apps and checkout?

I set up and configure the apps you need, and customize checkout where your plan allows. I’ll steer you away from apps you don’t.

How long does it take?

A refresh is often days; a full build runs two to three weeks. The calculator gives you a realistic timeline as you go.

Do you offer ongoing maintenance?

Yes, on a simple monthly plan — updates, backups, and small changes. No long contracts; cancel anytime.

What if I’m not on Shopify yet?

No problem — I’ll help you decide if it’s the right fit, set up the store, and migrate your content. That’s the migration add-on.
