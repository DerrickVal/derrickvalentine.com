### Get Ready To Stand Out From The Rest.

#### Web Design (UI/UX)**

Time to make this web thing easy! I work with each of my clients to find out who are you selling your art to then we create a visually appealing layout for them. Have you ever been to a site and you just find everything you are looking for instantly? Like when you load YouTube and go right to the song you were looking for? That’s UI and we will make it look fun and easy.

#### Web Development**

Its going to get a bit nerdy but that’s okay. This is the part of the process that you will hear the least from me but is also the most important. I will work on the backend of your site setting up security to protect from hackers, optimizing for page loading, and strategically building your sites architecture for SEO (basically how you rank on google).

#### Marketing Tools**

My entire goal of this venture is **to let artist get back to creating their art**. I find painters who I have worked with forget that they need a logo as well or their business cards are boring templates from staples because they needed to have something to hand out and the convention was in 3 days. I can help with that, from bringing your unique style to your gig proposal to just making sure all your content looks the same. I work with you to create beautiful and expressive constancy.
