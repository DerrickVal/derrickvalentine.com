# 58 agency and co

##### Creative Brief

#### Based in Portland, Oregon Shannon Brown started “58 | Agency & Co” in 2015 as a way to express his love for nature photography.

The most important part of building any artist brand is making sure their art is at the forefront. It is very easy for a marketing strategy to either compete with the product or for the brand identity to become boring and plain. I took inspiration from Shannon’s own style of photography to create a more modern gallery, choosing a “less is more” approach with an aim to create a easy to navigate UI that placed his work boldly on every page.

##### Result

#### Working as a two part plan, the website in pairing with sending live mail became the perfect strategy to secure several different jobs and positions.
