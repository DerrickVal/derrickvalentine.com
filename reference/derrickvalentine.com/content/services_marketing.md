## Marketing for Your Small Business

Here we will build everything the users interact with on the site. Starting from your specific brand identity (colors, imagery, mood) and build a website your users find intuitive to use and you find easy to maintain.

A good marketing service is focused on one thing—results. Our focus is never on “sugar-high” results from blindly overspending on ads and flash promos, but on strategic, sustainable results that gain momentum over time. That’s why we start each marketing service with a kick-off call guided with the goal of ensuring I understand your goals for your small business. From there, we map a tailored plan with transparent performance reporting every month so you see meaningful progress toward your goals each step of the way.

##### HOW WE DO IT

## My Working Process

![Kick off Call](https://derrickvalentine.com/file/2022/07/looks_one_white_48dp.svg)

#### Kick off Call

We will have an initial call to discuss your goals and the types of engagement you are looking for.

![Research](https://derrickvalentine.com/file/2022/10/looks_two_white_48dp.svg)

#### Research

Then I will go and learn all about your preferred customers, where they spend their free time and what convinces them to take action.

![Planning](https://derrickvalentine.com/file/2022/10/looks_3_white_48dp.svg)

#### Planning

A written plan will then be constructed with measurable results with edits from you to ensure we are on the same page.

![Engagement](https://derrickvalentine.com/file/2022/10/looks_4_white_48dp.svg)

#### Engagement

Now with our plan in hand we will implement it while constantly checking to make sure we are meeting our previously agreed results.

#### Create A Tailored Plan

Together we will create an easy-to-understand marketing plan that is tailored to your business. Each plan is tailored to your budget to maximize the value of your dollars.
