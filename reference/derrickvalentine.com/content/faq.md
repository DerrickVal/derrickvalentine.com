FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

### I notice some similar questions I get. If these do not answer yours please email me.

#### How much does a website cost?**

As much as I would like to this question simply, there is no thumb rule as to what a website should cost. It will certainly depend on project requirements, features, and inclusions to say the least. Some websites that are less complex will incur a different cost than a website that has some custom features and integrations. A fully customized website with in-depth custom features and integrations including e-commerce will incur higher cost. We will have a detailed conversation to discuss your needs and provide the right quote.

#### Where are you located?**

I am based about 30 minutes outside of Washington D.C at the moment, however I have clients based in just about every state. Thanks to online portals like Zoom and Google Meet I can be available to you any time from 9 a.m. (est) to 8 p.m. (est).

#### What tools do you use to create websites?**

I can create your website with most tools from Squarespace to React & Node.Js. Most commonly I will create with WordPress or Webflow because they are great content management systems that my clients find easy to use and update.

#### When do I pay?**

For most projects, equal payments are made at the start,and again at the end of the project, but I can work with you to set up a schedule that meets your needs. Websites can be a big investment and I want to help you budget for the expense in whatever way possible. I accept checks, PayPal, and all major credit cards.

#### Who writes the content for the site?**

You are the expert on your business, so it’s usually best if it comes from you. If you need help, I can recommend copywriters who can help clean up or write original content for you at an additional cost.

#### Where should I host my website?**

There are lots of options but I host all of my clients. This is for a very simple reason. If something ever happens there are no delays. If you host with GoDaddy for example and we need to edit the server I will spend a lot of time on the phone talking to a rep from a data center. The whole time your site is down. By hosting with me, we have total control over everything.

#### What if I need help on my site down the road? **

I am only an email away! I am here to help you as much or as little as you need, and I do not disappear once the site is launched. I have been doing this for a long time and prefer to build long lasting relationships with my clients. I am going anywhere anytime soon.

#### How much input do I have in the process?**

A Ton! Throughout each part of the process I am constantly looking for feedback. Before a line of code is written, I will send you a mockup of the homepage design so you can see exactly what I am thinking before we get started. My rule is no surprises.

#### Will I be able to update the site myself when it’s finished?**

Yes! I love it when my clients take control of their website and learn to manage it on their own. I will provide you with a set of comprehensive video tutorials to help you learn your way around, as well provide you with a training session to help get you on your way. (Note: Feel free to sent me questions I love to answer them!)

#### Will you maintain my site for me?**

Yes I can! We can talk about how you would like your site to evolve over time. I have some clients who only want me to make sure their security is up to date and maintain updates. Others send me a word doc each week and I make sure their blog is updated and update pages as needed. Whatever your needs are, we can make a custom plan for you!
