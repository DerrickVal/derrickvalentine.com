## What Your Customers See First.

Unfortunately, your new targeted audience probably does not know much about you. Before they know about your business’s goals or mission, they see the label, or that flyer for your event. My purpose is to show off what makes your business special in the form of an eye-catching graphic.

## My Working Process

![Consultation](https://derrickvalentine.com/file/2022/07/looks_one_white_48dp.svg)

#### Consultation

We will start off having a conversation about your goals and designs that you like.

![Mock-up](https://derrickvalentine.com/file/2022/10/looks_two_white_48dp.svg)

#### Mock-up

I will then go to the drawing board and show you a few different ideas so we are on the same page.

![Review](https://derrickvalentine.com/file/2022/10/looks_3_white_48dp.svg)

#### Review

You will have time here to choose your favorite mockup, together we will edit and shape the design.

![Production](https://derrickvalentine.com/file/2022/10/looks_4_white_48dp.svg)

#### Production

After we have completed all edits, all files will be turned over to you for printing.

#### Brand Consistency

It is extremely important that all your designs look the same! Think about every major brand you have ever interacted with, Target, Walmart, Apple all of them have the same feel in their stores and marketing. Just because you are probably not as large does not mean you cannot have access to the same strategies. Even a wedding can maintain brand consistency and so can you.

#### Partnerships & Speed

Being a small business owner myself I know how nice it is to be able to pick up the phone and cross an item off my to-do list. The most important things are getting problems solved and speed, the faster the flyers get back to you the faster you can start marketing and the more people you can reach. I aim to empower you in the most effective ways possible.

#### Social Media

Your brand does not just stop with physical prints, in this day and age if you are not online you are missing access to roughly 4.4 billion people. That’s a lot of possible customers if you only get .0001% that is still 440,000 people! I offer services to help market to these people. My services range from taking over all of your accounts for you, writing content, creating post, and posting. To taking your words and creating the graphics for you to post on your own time.
